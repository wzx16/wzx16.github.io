Robotics · Learning · Manipulation

Zixing Wang

Pronounced Zee-Shing

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Professor Ahmed H. Qureshi. Previously, I earned an M.S. in Robotics and a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota, where I worked with Professor Nikolaos Papanikolopoulos.

My research focuses on world-action models, generative policy steering, and robotic manipulation with heterogeneous systems.
