Zixing Wang 王自兴

Welcome! I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Prof. Ahmed H. Qureshi. Previously, I received my M.S. in Robotics and B.A. in Computer Science from the University of Minnesota, where I worked with Prof. Nikolaos Papanikolopoulos.

My research focuses on robotic manipulation, with an emphasis on world-action models, generative policy steering, and soft-body manipulation.

Zixing is pronounced Zee-Shing.
